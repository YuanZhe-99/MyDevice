# Device thumbnail sources

Every PNG here is a derivative of a photograph or render, freely licensed unless its row says
otherwise. Each one was produced with
`dart run tool/prepare_device_image.dart <source> <output.png>`, which removes the plain
background (or, with `--roundrect`, masks a tightly cropped phone; or, with `--keep-background`,
leaves an already transparent render alone), trims to the device, and fits it inside the avatar
circle on a transparent 256 px canvas; `--crop` first picks one region of a larger source, such as
one device of a colour line-up. `tool/validate_json.dart` checks that the result is transparent and circle-safe.

Since 1.6.0 some rows are **not freely licensed**: ASUS NUC product renders, manufacturer product
images supplied by the repository owner (Razer Blade 14, Intel NUC 11, Galaxy Z Fold6 and Fold8,
AirPods 4), and since 1.6.1 Apple product renders from Apple's tech-specs pages (MacBook Air/Pro,
iPad mini/Pro, iPhone 14/15 Pro/17/Air, Apple Watch, AirPods Pro 2) and Microsoft Store renders
(Windows Dev Kit 2023, Surface Laptop 7). They name the copyright holder so they can be replaced
when a free image turns up.

**Trademarks.** Every picture shows a commercial product, and most show its maker's name or
logo. Those names, logos and product designs are trademarks of their owners, listed per row under
"Trademarks shown". They are used only to identify the device a record describes; their use
implies no affiliation with or endorsement by the owner.

**Not covered by the GPL.** These images are not part of MyDevice's GPL-3.0 source. Each keeps
the license in its row. Rows marked **not freely licensed** are the copyright holder's product
images, included for identification without a license; they will be removed or replaced if the
copyright holder asks.

Derivatives keep the original license. "Shows" names what the photo actually depicts when one
image serves several near-identical templates.
