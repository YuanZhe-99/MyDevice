- `v1.5.0`: The adaptive-layout foundation for foldables, tablets and desktop windows — the first of six releases that bring every screen onto one set of rules, adopted wholesale from the conventions MyAnime worked out in its 1.5.2–1.5.6 releases. **A new policy module, `lib/shared/utils/adaptive_layout.dart`, imports nothing but `dart:core` and holds every width decision the app makes.** Its split rule `canSplitLayout` is an aspect test, not a width breakpoint, and the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is why: it unfolds to a 4:3 *landscape* panel, so in portrait it is 3:4 and narrower relative to its height than the near-square Fold 7 it replaced, and one device needs two answers at one width — width ≥ 600, height ≥ 480 and width/height ≥ 0.82 give it two panes in landscape and the original single column in portrait. No page splits yet; the rule lands first so 1.5.1's lists and 1.5.2's detail pages gate on something already tested. **The shell moves navigation to the side from 600 logical pixels**: a `NavigationRail` (`groupAlignment: 0`, scrollable at compact heights) built from the same destination list as the bottom bar, chosen by `useNavigationRail`, a second, width-only rule kept deliberately apart from the split rule — a phone in landscape at 915 × 412 cannot split but spends a fifth of its height on a bottom bar while 915 dp of width sit idle, and a Z Fold 8 in portrait gets a rail for the same reason. MyAnime's bottom-bar inset was deliberately not ported: MyDevice's tab pages each bring their own `Scaffold` under the shell's, so a page body never sits under the bar, and the device list's `bottom: 80` is clearance for its three floating action buttons. **The three inline breakpoints the app had are gone**, each now a named predicate with its number justified in a doc comment: the services overview's metric grid goes through `serviceMetricColumns`, arithmetically identical to the inline rule so no viewport changed; the topology card's `< 680` becomes `useTopologyActionsRow` at the same 680, but the card is now handed the width left after the rail, so a Pixel 10 Pro Fold in portrait stacks its actions under the title where it used to row them; and the finance summary's `>= 520 ? 3 : 2` becomes `financeSummaryColumns`, a `columnCapacity` at 160 dp floored at two (the card never stacked one metric per row and a cover screen must not start), whose third column arrives at 512 rather than 520. **Both online-search dialogs derive their height from the window** instead of a fixed 560 / 480 that overflowed a phone or a folded cover screen held in landscape: `dialogBodyHeight` leaves 40 above and below, stops at the old sizes where there is room (a Fold 8 in landscape is unchanged) and never drops below 240; width is capped at Material's 560, which also stops the dialog stretching across a tablet. - `v1.5.1`: Lists that use a wide screen. Once a window passes 1.5.0's split rule, the device, network and dataset lists and the services page's devices / routes / ports views fill it with as many columns of a stated minimum width as fit, capped at four, laid out left to right then top to bottom — Google's adaptive-minimum-width feed guidance through `columnCapacity`, never a hardcoded count per breakpoint. **Each tile brings its own minimum, justified in its doc comment**: 320 dp for a device card (152 of avatar, gap and trailing chrome leaves 168 for a twenty-character name and a two-line subtitle), 300 for a network card (a single-line "type · subnet" subtitle), 320 for a dataset tile (four storage lines that must not wrap) and 320 for the services cards. The minimums are what make one width answer differently per list: a tablet in landscape gives networks three columns where the others get two, and a Z Fold 7 in portrait leaves the device list three logical pixels short of a second column while the dataset list beside it gets one — the rule working at its boundary. `listColumnCount` takes the minimum as a parameter, the one departure from MyAnime's version, because MyDevice has four tile shapes. **Each list remembers its own count** in `storage_config.json` (`deviceListColumns`, `networkListColumns`, `dataSetListColumns`, `serviceListColumns`), device-locally because window size is a property of the device and not of the account, defaulting to auto — removed from the file, not written as zero — and clamped rather than rewritten when the window shrinks, so a count set on a desktop survives a fold and comes back on unfolding; the app-bar control is hidden, not disabled, wherever the width fits only one column, and in reorder mode and on the services overview, which is a heterogeneous scroll and deliberately stays single-column. Content width is `shellContentWidth` less each page's own padding, so the rail is paid for. **Gestures follow the columns**: the device and dataset tiles keep swipe-to-edit and swipe-to-delete at one column and drop them above it, because a horizontal drag inside one narrow cell is ambiguous; the trailing chevron becomes a menu carrying the same actions — the menu the services tiles already had — and since delete has no other entrance on either page, that menu is what keeps it reachable.
